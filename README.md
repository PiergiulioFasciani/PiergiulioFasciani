#### 📌 Featured work

**[Supermarket Entry Effects on Urban Retail: An Empirical Pilot Study in Milan](https://github.com/PiergiulioFasciani/thesisbsc_supermarkets)** · R
BSc thesis, which grew out of my IGIER research apprenticeship (supervised by Prof. Jan David Bakker).

*Does a new supermarket kill the corner shops around it?* In Milan, apparently not.
- **Data:** a 2015–2025 quarterly panel built by parsing the full **OpenStreetMap edit history** to recover store entries and exits (375 treated and 367 control sites, 1M+ observations)
- **Design:** stacked, staggered event study estimated via **PPML** against not-yet-treated sites, with treatment radii of 500, 750 and 1,000 m
- **Result:** supermarket entry is associated with a **+7.5%** rise in the local stock of small food retailers once a 6-quarter anticipation window is modelled (baseline **+4.7%**). The pattern is consistent with agglomeration, not crowding out.
- **Robustness:** pre-trend Wald tests, an anticipation-shifted specification and **HonestDiD** sensitivity analysis (Rambachan & Roth, 2023). The stock effect holds up to M̄ = 0.2; entry and exit flows are too noisy to call.
